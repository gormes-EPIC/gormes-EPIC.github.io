# "No Silver Bullets" Discussion

## Your Task

Read the article [No Silver Bullets: Essence and Accidents in Software Engineering](https://www.cgl.ucsf.edu/Outreach/pc204/NoSilverBullet.html) and be prepared to discuss the following questions in class. 

1. Define essential vs accidental difficulties as it relates to the text
2. What four qualities define the essence of software systems and what do they mean?
3. What is a quote or phrase that stuck out to you?
4. What things do you forsee making the biggest impact on essential problems programmers face?
5. Are modern LLMs as we know them the "silver bullet" described in the article or do they only address accidental problems?

Then, read [There Is Still No Silver Bullet](https://cekrem.github.io/posts/there-is-still-no-silver-bullet/) as an argument. 


6. Re-examine the question: are modern LLMs as we know them the "silver bullet" described in the article or do they only address accidental problems? Did the new article change your perspective?
