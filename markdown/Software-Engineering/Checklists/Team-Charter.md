# Team Charter: Checklist

> **The goal:** agree as a team on your project goal, what "done" looks like, how you'll treat each other, and how you'll work together, then write it all down in a team charter.

## Step 1: Get Set Up

- [ ] Sit together with your whole team.
- [ ] Create a shared document for your team charter, and share it with every team member.
- [ ] Add a title and everyone's name at the top.

## Step 2: Meet Your Team and Set a Goal

- [ ] Write down each team member's name and role.
- [ ] Define your project goal in one or two sentences.
- [ ] Describe what success looks like, and how you'll know you've reached it. List 2–3 things you could actually check, like "a user can log in."
- [ ] List each team member's main responsibilities and deliverables (the things they'll finish and show).

## Step 3: Choose Your Group Values

- [ ] Talk about groups you've been in that were successful and unsuccessful. What made the difference?
- [ ] Have each person choose three qualities of a good group.
- [ ] Compare your choices and agree on a top three.
- [ ] **For each value**, write:
  - [ ] a quick definition of what it means to your team
  - [ ] what you'll do if it doesn't happen

## Step 4: Plan How You'll Work Together

- [ ] Decide how you'll contact each other. If needed, share contact information.
- [ ] Decide how someone will let the team know they're stuck, falling behind, or need help.
- [ ] Decide what happens when a team member is absent, and what the absent person is responsible for.
- [ ] Decide how you'll make decisions (for example, consensus or majority vote).
- [ ] Have each person name a strength they'll bring to this project.
- [ ] Have each person name an area they want to grow in during this project.
- [ ] Have each person share anything else the team should know about them to be successful.

## Step 5: Decide on Development Standards

Write your team's decision for each question.

- [ ] What branching strategy will we use? For example, main plus feature branches, or a develop branch.
- [ ] How will we name branches? For example, `feature/login-page` or `bugfix/cart-total`.
- [ ] What format will commit messages follow?
- [ ] How often should we commit and push?
- [ ] Can anyone push directly to `main`, or does everything go through a pull request?
- [ ] Who resolves merge conflicts, and how?
- [ ] What language conventions will we follow, such as `camelCase` or `snake_case`?
- [ ] How will we organize folders and files?
- [ ] When and how will we write comments or documentation?

## Step 6: Review and Submit

- [ ] Read the whole charter again and make sure every question has an answer the whole team agrees on.
- [ ] Proofread it. It should be clear enough that someone outside your team could understand it.
- [ ] Have every team member confirm they agree to follow it.
- [ ] Submit your team charter on Google Classroom.
- [ ] Keep the charter somewhere easy to find. You'll come back to it whenever your team needs to make a decision or work through a problem.
