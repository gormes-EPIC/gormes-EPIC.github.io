# Team Charter

## Objective
1. Set a goal for the project and what "done" looks like
2. Create group norms and establish communication channels

## Your Task

1. As a team, answer the following questions in a shared document.

    1. What are your team names and roles?
    2. Define your project goal.
    3. What does success look like and how will you know you have reached it?
    4. List each team member's primary responsibilities and deliverables.
    5. Choose three group values that will define how you will treat your group mates.
        1. Think about qualities of groups you have been in that were successful and unsuccessful.
        2. Then, have each person choose three qualities.
        3. Compare your choices and choose a top three.
        4. **For each** write down a quick definition of what that means and what you are going to do if it doesn't happen.
    6. Decide how you will contact each other. If needed, share contact information.
    7. How will you indicate if you are stuck, falling behind, or need some help?
    8. What will you do if one of your team members is missing? What is their responsibility as the one who is absent?
    9. How will you make decisions? (consensus, majority vote, etc.)
    10. Have each person name a strength of theirs they will bring to this project. Have each person name an area they want to grow in during this project.
    11. Is there anything else your team needs to know about you to be successful?
    12. Decide on development standards.
        1. What branching strategy will we use? For example, main plus feature branches, or a develop branch.
        2. How will we name branches? For example, `feature/login-page` or `bugfix/cart-total`.
        3. What format will commit messages follow?
        4. How often should we commit and push?
        5. Can anyone push directly to `main`, or does everything go through a pull request?
        6. Who resolves merge conflicts, and how?
        7. What language conventions will we follow, such as `camelCase` or `snake_case`?
        8. How will we organize folders and files?
        9. When and how do we write comments or documentation?

## Rubric
- **30 points** - Delivered well-written, clear team charter. 
- **0 points** - Missing or incomplete
