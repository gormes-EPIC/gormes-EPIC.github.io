# Visualizing AI Activity

## Objectives

1. Distinguish between a hand-coded algorithm and a system that learns from experience.
2. Explain the roles of an agent, its environment, actions, and rewards.
3. Run a training process and observe how performance changes across generations or episodes.
4. Plot and interpret training curves showing best and average scores over time.
Use summary statistics to evaluate whether the AI is improving.
5. Explain how changing a training setting (such as population size or mutation rate) affects learning.
