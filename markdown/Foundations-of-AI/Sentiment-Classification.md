# Sentiment Classifcation with an LSTM

## Objectives
1. Preprocess text by cleaning, tokenizing, and padding sequences for model input.
2. Explain how recurrent networks process sequences and why LSTMs are better at remembering earlier information.
3. Build and train an LSTM model that classifies text as positive or negative.
4. Test the model on new text and analyze where it struggles, such as with sarcasm or negation.
