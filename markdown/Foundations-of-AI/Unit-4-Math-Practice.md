# Unit 4 Math Practice

## Objectives
1. Explain how vector embeddings represent words or documents as points in space, with similar meanings placed closer together.
2. Calculate cosine similarity between two vectors and interpret what the score means.
3. Use set operations (union, intersection, difference) to describe relationships between groups of data.
4. Apply logical operators (AND, OR, NOT) and conditional statements to evaluate decision rules like those used by AI agents.
