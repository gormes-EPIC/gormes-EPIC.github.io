### Question 10

1. Substitute the known values into the equation: $d = (0)(6) + \frac{1}{2}(4)(6^2)$.
2. Add the initial velocity term (which is zero) to get the final answer: $d = 0 + 72 = 72$ meters.
3. Identify the known values: initial velocity ($u = 0 m/s$), acceleration ($a = 4 m/s^2$), time ($t = 6 s$).
4. Multiply acceleration by the squared time and halve it: $\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 36 = 72$.
5. Choose the appropriate kinematic equation: $\text{distance} = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$.
6. Calculate the squared term: $6^2 = 36$.
