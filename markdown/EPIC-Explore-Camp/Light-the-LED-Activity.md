## Objective

1. Wire a circuit using a Raspberry Pi and a breadboard
2. Create a Python program to make an LED blink

## Materials 

<img src="/assets/light-the-led-activity-figure-1.png">

Your box will contain:

- Raspberry Pi with T-cobbler and breadboard
- Power cord
- HDMI to M
- 2 M-M jumper wires
- 5mm LED
- Resistor (over 130 Ohms)

## Setting Up Your Pi

1. Plug the power cord into an outlet and the other end into your Pi's USB-C port
2. Plug the HDMI cord into your monitor and the Micro HDMI end into your Pi
3. Grab a mouse and keyboard and plug each of them into the black USB-A ports on your Pi
